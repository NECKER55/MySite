🤖 How it Works

This AI agent runs daily automated predictions to forecast future hardware component prices. The system continuously learns and updates its forecasts by analyzing multiple market factors:

1. Multi-Source Data Collection: The agent scrapes pricing data from major retailers, marketplaces, and e-commerce platforms every 24 hours.
2. Market Analysis: Analyzes supply chain trends, product launch cycles, cryptocurrency mining demand, seasonal patterns, and global chip shortages.
3. News Sentiment Analysis: Natural language processing evaluates tech news sentiment to detect emerging price-affecting events.
4. Daily Price Forecasting: Machine learning models predict price movements for the next 30-90 days based on historical patterns and current market conditions.
5. Automatic Updates: Predictions are recalculated and updated every day to reflect the latest market data and trends.
